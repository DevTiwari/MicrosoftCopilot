Model strategy

Multi-model by design.

The enterprise value is not limited to the model. It comes from the context, orchestration, application skills, agents and controls around the model.

OpenAI models

General reasoning, creation and analysis in supported Microsoft experiences.

Anthropic models

Available in supported Microsoft Copilot, Copilot Studio and Microsoft 365 experiences, subject to administrator settings and regional considerations.

Microsoft capabilities

Purpose-built models, orchestration, Work IQ and application skills optimized for enterprise work.

Start with value

Four high-value work patterns.

Select scenarios where business impact, data readiness and sponsorship come together.

Customer meeting intelligence

Bring together account context, recent interactions, open actions and external insight.

Outcome: More relevant customer conversations and faster follow-up.

Proposal and content creation

Draft from approved sources, templates and prior work, then refine inside business applications.

Outcome: Faster first drafts with better consistency and collaboration.

Data analysis and decision support

Analyze workbooks and business data, identify patterns and create decision-ready outputs.

Outcome: Shorter analysis cycles and clearer recommendations.

Workflow and agent automation

Use agents to retrieve context, apply rules, trigger actions and keep people in control.

Outcome: Reduced manual coordination and more predictable execution.
